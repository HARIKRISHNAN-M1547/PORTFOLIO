Desktop pet · Phone controlled

ORBY

A small desktop pet with animated OLED eyes. It shows the time and day, reacts to touch, and you change its eye mode from your phone over Wi-Fi.

(videos/orby.mp4)

OverviewWhat Orby does

Orby sits on your desk and keeps you company. An ESP8266 (Wemos D1 Mini) draws expressive eyes on a small OLED screen and also shows the clock and the day. Orby hosts a web page, so you connect your phone over Wi-Fi, open the page and switch the eye mode. A touch sensor lets you interact with it by hand, and a rechargeable battery with a USB-C charger makes it cable-free.

ObjectivesWhat we set out to build

  • Build a small, friendly desktop pet with lively animated eyes on an OLED display.
  • Control Orby's eye mode from a phone through a web page hosted by the ESP8266.
  • Show the clock time and day on the display, so Orby is useful as well as fun.
  • Add a touch sensor so Orby responds when you touch it.
  • Make it portable with a rechargeable Li-ion battery and a USB-C charging module.
