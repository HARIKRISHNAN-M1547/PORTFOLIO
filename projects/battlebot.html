BATTLE BOT

A 4-wheel battle car you drive from a web page on your phone, over the robot's own Wi-Fi. Powered by an ESP8266 and an L298N motor driver.

OverviewWhat the Battle Bot does

The Battle Bot is a small combat robot car built for the arena. The ESP8266 creates its own Wi-Fi network and serves a control web page. You join the network, open the page on your phone, and your taps are sent to the ESP8266, which switches the L298N motor driver to drive the left and right wheels. A wedge plate at the front and a rear wing complete the battle design.

ESP8266Wi-Fi microcontroller
L298Ndual H-bridge motor driver
4WDfour geared motors, left/right steering
Phoneweb-page control over its own Wi-Fi

ObjectivesWhat we set out to build

  • Build a compact battle robot that is controlled wirelessly from a web page on a mobile phone, with no router or internet needed.
  • Use an ESP8266 for its built-in Wi-Fi and web server, and an L298N driver to run the motors from a battery pack.
  • Give the robot four-wheel drive with differential steering for quick turns in the arena.
  • Protect the electronics with a wedge front plate and keep the build light and low-cost.
